# OA VENTRAAL — Onderarm (ventraal / anterior)

**Algemeen (oppervlakkig):** O = epicondylus medialis humeri · N = n. medianus  
\* uitzondering: n. ulnaris

---

## A) Oppervlakkig

| M | O | I | F | N |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| m. pronator teres | C.H. epicondylus med. humeri; C.U. proc. coronoideus ulnae | facies lateralis radii | pronatie OA | n. medianus |
| m. flexor carpi radialis | epicondylus med. humeri | os metacarpale II–III | flexie OA; radiale deviatie | n. medianus |
| m. palmaris longus | epicondylus med. humeri | aponeurosis palmaris | palmairflexie / flexie OA | n. medianus |
| m. flexor carpi ulnaris | C.H. epicondylus med. humeri; C.U. olecranon | os pisiforme / os hamatum / os metacarpale V | palmairflexie / flexie OA | **n. ulnaris** \* |
| m. flexor digitorum superficialis | C.H. epicondylus med. humeri; C.R. facies anterior radii | digiti II–V | flexie OA / palmairflexie / flexie MCP + PIP | n. medianus |

## B) Diep

| M | O | I | F | N |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| m. flexor digitorum profundus | facies medialis + anterior ulnae / membrana interossea | distale phalanx digiti 2–5 | palmairflexie / flexie MCP, PIP, DIP | n. medianus + **n. ulnaris** \* |
| m. flexor pollicis longus | facies anterior radii / membrana interossea | distale phalanx duim | palmairflexie / flexie CMC, MCP, IP | n. medianus |
| m. pronator quadratus | facies anterior ulnae | facies anterior radii | pronatie | n. medianus |

### Afkortingen
- **OA** = onderarm  
- **C.H.** = caput humerale · **C.U.** = caput ulnare · **C.R.** = caput radiale  
- **MCP / PIP / DIP** = metacarpofalangeaal / proximaal interfalangeaal / distaal interfalangeaal
